      Hyard Records · long

      Hyard Records is an independent label for dark electronic music and the sounds in between, founded in 2024 by Blurfnes, the electronic project of Lovro Bedenk. It started as a home for his own releases and the collaborations around them, and grew into a small label with a clear sound: hard synth basses, raw analog textures and the build-ups of the rave floor. The catalog runs from techno and progressive to tracks that cross into orchestral and trap territory, with every release numbered from HYD-001.
