LABEL — LONG BIO
Hyard Records is an independent label for dark electronic music and the sounds in between, founded
in 2024 by Blurfnes, the electronic project of Lovro Bedenk. It started as a home for his
own releases and the collaborations around them, and grew into a small label with a clear
sound: hard synth basses, raw analog textures and the build-ups of the rave floor. The
catalog runs from techno and progressive to tracks that cross into orchestral and trap
territory, with 29 releases numbered from HYD-001 by Blurfnes, Rave Saint and Nick.

ARTIST — BLURFNES
Blurfnes is a solo Croatian electronic music project created by Lovro Bedenk in 2023,
recognized for merging orchestral elements with analog synthesizers and heavy bass
influences rooted in trap. With six years of music school studying oboe and piano,
including two years of orchestra experience and over 30 solo performances, Blurfnes brings
a strong classical foundation into electronic production. Blurfnes now studies audio
engineering and sound design, refining both technical and creative approaches to sound.
In 2024, Blurfnes founded Hyard Records and frequently collaborates with Rave Saint and
Blueexnetic. The sound of Blurfnes combines orchestral textures with hard synth basses and
raw analog synthesis, evolving from an early focus on trap into the development of a
personal genre while also contributing to various subgenres of techno with No Time to
Waste and Mainstage Suspension. Live performances center on a live looping setup heavily
inspired by Paul Kalkbrenner, emphasizing real-time composition, gradual build-ups, and an
immersive, performance-driven approach to electronic music.

ARTIST — RAVE SAINT
Rave Saint is the techno project of Dominik, whose music started early: music school as a
kid, trumpet in school bands, then writing and rapping as Q-Dom, both solo and as one half
of the duo Colorz. Through his architecture studies he moved into design and software
development, branding well-known names in the Slovenian music scene while his own music
waited.
Once his business was on solid ground, he found his way back through the rave scene, first
as a fan. He picked up Ableton, used the pandemic years to teach himself production, and
built up a backlog of demos. The turning point came at EXIT festival 2022, where he
proposed to his girlfriend during the last minute of Boris Brejcha's set. The moment went
viral, and that season became his debut single, Rave Saint, featuring her vocals and
released in December 2022.
Spotify: https://open.spotify.com/artist/52FisLP1SHHdWWk5LBF9Ll

ARTIST — NICK.
nick. is a Croatian producer working across hardstyle, metal, rock and video game music
(VGM). At first glance these genres don't have much in common, but for him they share one
idea: music with energy, character and a clear reason to exist.
He has stayed relatively anonymous. He doesn't build a story around himself and lets the
music speak for him instead. Today he releases rarely, putting out only the tracks he
considers masterpieces. That's why his catalogue isn't tied to one sound or genre. Every
release stands on its own, as a separate project that had to earn its place.
He composes and produces in FL Studio 26, with VST instruments and soundfonts. Producers
often look down on soundfonts as an outdated tool, but nick. uses them on purpose. That
combination is where his distinctive identity comes from: a sound that carries the spirit
of video game music with the weight and punch of hardstyle and metal.
His singles You Are an Idiot (2025) and Miss Me (2026) are out on Hyard Records.
