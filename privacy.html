    Privacy policy

    Last updated 21 November 2026

    Hyard Records runs this website to share our releases and radio, receive demos and offer production, mixing and mastering. We don't sell data, we don't run ads, and we don't use tracking cookies. This page explains the small amount of personal data that is still involved when you visit, and what you can do about it.

    1. Who we are

    This website is operated by Hyard Records ("we", "us"). We are the controller of personal data processed through this website.

    Contact: our contact page or info@hyardrecords.com
    Postal address: Ulica Hermana Bužana 10, 10000 Zagreb, Croatia

    2. What we collect and why

    Visiting the website

    When you open any page, your browser automatically sends technical information to our hosting provider, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request. The server records this in log files so the site can be delivered and protected against abuse.

    • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
    • Retention: we don't keep these logs ourselves and can't see them. GitHub, our hosting provider, records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes and keeps them as described in its privacy statement.

    Artist accounts

    If you create an artist account, we collect your name, stage name (if you give one), email address and password, or the name and email from your Google account if you sign in with Google. In your profile you can add your phone number, country and Spotify, SoundCloud, Instagram and YouTube links. We use these to run your account, review your demos and deliver services you book. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and we never see it.

    • Legal basis: performing our agreement with you, the account and the services you request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
    • Retention: as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in your dashboard under Security; your profile, demos and their files are then deleted.

    Demo submissions

    When you send a demo from your dashboard, we store its title, artist name, genre, BPM, key, any link and message you add, the audio files you upload and the status of your demo. Only you and the Hyard Records team can see them.

    • Legal basis: steps at your request before a possible agreement, such as signing a release (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), and your consent, which you give with the checkbox on the form (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).
    • Retention: we delete a demo and its files as soon as we've made our decision and emailed it to you. If we sign it, we keep what we need for the release agreement. You can withdraw a demo we haven't started on, delete your account, or ask us to delete it sooner at any time.

    If you email us directly instead, we receive your email address and whatever you include, and handle it the same way.

    Paid services (production, mixing, mastering)

    When you book a service, we store your order: what you bought, the price, the order status, the brief and files you upload and the finished files we deliver. Payment is handled by Stripe: you enter your card or PayPal details on Stripe's secure page and we never see them. Stripe tells us whether the payment succeeded and gives us a payment reference.

    • Legal basis: performing our agreement with you (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), and our legal duty to keep accounting records (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR).
    • Retention: order and invoice records are kept for 11 years, as Croatian accounting law requires. If you delete your account, paid orders are kept for that period without your name and email. Uploaded and finished files are deleted when we close your order, once you've downloaded the finished files.
    • Payment processor: Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. (Ireland). See Stripe's privacy policy. Stripe also checks payments for fraud as an independent controller.

    Newsletter and album pre-saves

    The newsletter and pre-save boxes aren't connected yet. Anything typed into them is checked in your browser and not sent or stored anywhere.

    If you sign up for the newsletter or pre-save an album, we collect your email address and which list you joined. We use it only to send you label news and updates about that release. You first get an email asking you to confirm, and every email has an unsubscribe link.

    • Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time by unsubscribing.
    • Retention: until you unsubscribe, after which your address is removed from our lists.
    • Processor: MailerLite (UAB MailerLite, Lithuania, EU) stores the list and sends the emails on our behalf. It may record whether emails are opened or links clicked. See MailerLite's privacy policy.

    Searching releases

    The release search runs entirely in your browser. What you type is not sent to us or stored.

    Radio player

    The radio section shows our Spotify playlist. The Spotify player only loads after you click "Load the player". Once loaded, Spotify receives your IP address and may set its own cookies, as described in Spotify's privacy policy. Until you click, nothing is sent to Spotify.

    3. Third-party services

    Some parts of the site load content from other companies. When that happens, your browser connects to them directly and they receive your IP address.

    • Google Fonts (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC) provides our typefaces. See Google's privacy policy.
    • Firebase (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC) runs artist accounts, the database, file storage for demos and orders, and the small server functions behind checkout. Data is stored in the EU (Belgium). See Firebase privacy and security.
    • Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd., Ireland) handles payments for services. See Stripe's privacy policy.
    • MailerLite (UAB MailerLite, Lithuania) manages our newsletter and pre-save lists and sends those emails.
    • Google Workspace (Google) is where we receive emails you send us, and it sends our order receipts and demo updates.
    • Spotify (Spotify AB) is the source of our release information, and release links open on Spotify. Once you're on Spotify, its own privacy policy applies.
    • Hosting: GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA) stores and serves this website. See GitHub's privacy statement.

    Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, transfers rely on an adequacy decision (such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) or standard contractual clauses.

    Our release catalog is updated automatically from Spotify's public catalog. This uses public release information only, not data about visitors.

    4. Cookies and local storage

    This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking technology. That's why there is no cookie banner. The only exception is the Spotify player in the radio section, which you load yourself and which may set Spotify's own cookies.

    This website does not set cookies and does not use advertising or tracking technology, so there is no cookie banner. To understand which pages and releases people visit, we use Plausible Analytics (Plausible Insights OÜ, Estonia, EU), which counts visits without cookies and without storing personal data or IP addresses. It records the page, the referring website, your country and your device type, plus clicks on outbound links and file downloads, only as anonymous totals. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See Plausible's data policy. The Spotify player in the radio section, which you load yourself, may set Spotify's own cookies.

    This website does not set cookies and does not use advertising or tracking technology, so there is no cookie banner. To understand which pages people visit, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics (Cloudflare, Inc.), which counts visits without cookies and without fingerprinting. It processes the page address, referring website, browser and country to produce anonymous totals. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in improving the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See Cloudflare's privacy policy. The Spotify player in the radio section, which you load yourself, may set Spotify's own cookies.

    This site remembers your language choice (English or Croatian) in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.

    If you log in, Firebase keeps you signed in by storing a sign-in token in your browser's local storage, and the site notes there whether you're logged in and how many items are in your cart, so the menu can show it. These are strictly necessary for the account you asked for, so they don't need a cookie banner. Logging out removes them. Stripe's payment page sets its own cookies to process the payment and prevent fraud.

    5. Your rights

    Under the GDPR you have the right to:

    • access the personal data we hold about you
    • have inaccurate data corrected
    • have your data deleted
    • restrict or object to how we process it
    • receive your data in a portable format
    • withdraw consent at any time, where processing relies on consent

    To use any of these rights, write to us using our contact page or at privacy@hyardrecords.com. We'll reply within one month.

    You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular the one in the EU country where you live or work, or where you think the problem happened.

    6. Children

    This website isn't aimed at children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

    7. Changes

    We may update this policy when the website or the law changes. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
