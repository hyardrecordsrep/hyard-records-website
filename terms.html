    Terms of service

    Last updated 23 November 2026

    These terms apply to Hyard Records artist accounts, to the production, mixing and mastering services you can book on this website, and to the digital products you can buy here, such as drum kits and sample packs. Please read them before you pay. The provider is the operator named in our legal notice.

    1. Artist accounts

    Accounts are free. Give accurate details and keep your password to yourself. You're responsible for what happens in your account. We can close accounts that are used for spam, abuse or to upload music the uploader doesn't own.

    2. Demos

    Sending a demo is free and doesn't create any obligation on either side. Only send music you wrote and own, including all samples. We listen to every demo but can't promise feedback. If we want to release your music, we'll offer you a separate written release agreement. Sending a demo never transfers any rights to us.

    3. Services and prices

    The services, what's included, the turnaround and the number of revision rounds are described on each service in your dashboard. Prices are in euros and are shown before you pay. [VAT: "Prices include VAT" or "No VAT is charged (not registered for VAT)"]. Per-track prices apply to one track of any length; packages cover the number of tracks shown.

    4. Order and payment

    You place an order by paying through Stripe Checkout. The agreement is made when the payment is confirmed. You'll see the order in your dashboard and get a receipt by email. We accept the payment methods shown on the Stripe page, such as cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal.

    5. Your files and the brief

    After paying, add a brief and upload your files (stems, project files or references) on the order in your dashboard. Work starts once we have everything we need, and turnaround times count from then. We'll confirm your slot and keep the order status up to date.

    6. Revisions and delivery

    Each service includes the revision rounds shown. A revision is a set of change requests on the delivered version, sent together. Extra rounds or a new direction can be booked separately. Finished files are delivered in your dashboard as WAV (and MP3 where included).

    When the order is complete, we delete the files you uploaded and the finished files from our systems. Download your finished files as soon as they're delivered: once the order is closed, we can't send them again.

    7. Right of withdrawal (consumers in the EU)

    If you book as a consumer, you can withdraw from the agreement within 14 days of the order without giving a reason. To withdraw, tell us clearly in writing through our contact page [or by email to the label address].

    At checkout you ask us to start work within the withdrawal period. That means:

    • if you withdraw before the work is finished, we refund what you paid minus a fair amount for the work already done;
    • once the service is fully delivered, the right of withdrawal ends.

    Refunds go back to the payment method you used, within 14 days of your withdrawal.

    Digital products work differently: see section 10.

    8. Cancellations and refunds

    Outside the withdrawal period, you can cancel an order before work starts for a full refund. Once work has started, we refund the part of the work not yet done. If we can't deliver for reasons on our side, you get a full refund for the undelivered part.

    9. Rights and credits

    You keep all rights in your music. Once the order is paid in full, you may use the delivered production, mix or master on your releases without paying anything more. Every service you buy must be credited to Lovro Bedenk on the release, in the release credits and on streaming platforms wherever credits can be added: for example "Produced by Lovro Bedenk", "Mixed by Lovro Bedenk" or "Mastered by Lovro Bedenk". We may mention the work and play short excerpts as part of our portfolio unless you ask us not to.

    You confirm that you have the right to use everything you send us, including samples, and that it doesn't infringe anyone else's rights.

    10. Digital products

    Drum kits, sample packs and our other digital products are sold as downloads. Each product in your dashboard describes what's inside, and most have a preview you can play before buying.

    Delivery: as soon as Stripe confirms your payment, we email you a download link from product@hyardrecords.com. The same link is on the order in your dashboard. Each link works up to 10 times. If you run out, or a file is damaged or incomplete, write to us and we'll send you a new one.

    Licence: when you buy a product, you get a personal, non-exclusive, worldwide licence to use its sounds in your own music. You can release, sell, stream, perform and license that music, including commercially, without paying anything more and without crediting us (a credit is always welcome).

    • resell, share, give away or upload the files or the sounds on their own, changed or unchanged;
    • put the sounds in another sample pack, drum kit, preset bank or sound library;
    • register the sounds on their own with Content ID or a similar system, or claim them as your own work.

    The licence is yours alone and can't be passed on to anyone else. Hyard Records keeps all other rights in the sounds. If you break these rules, the licence ends.

    Withdrawal: because a download is delivered straight away, at checkout you ask us to deliver it immediately and agree that you lose your right of withdrawal once it's delivered. If a product doesn't download, doesn't work or isn't as described, we'll fix it, replace it or refund you.

    11. Liability

    We do our work with care and as described. We're not liable for indirect loss or lost profits, except where the law doesn't allow this to be limited, such as for intent, gross negligence, or injury to life, body or health. Keep your own backups of what you send us.

    12. Complaints

    If something isn't right, contact us first through our contact page. We reply to written complaints within 15 days. See the legal notice for out-of-court dispute resolution.

    13. Law

    Croatian law applies. If you're a consumer living in another EU country, you keep the protection of the mandatory consumer law of that country.

    14. Changes

    We may update these terms. The version that applies to an order is the one shown when you paid.
