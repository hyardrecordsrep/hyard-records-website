    Send your demo

    Demo guidelines

    Read these before you send. It helps us listen properly.

    1. 01

      Our sound

      Techno, rave, progressive and the electronic music around them. If it would work on a dark dancefloor, we want to hear it.

    2. 02

      Original and unreleased

      Only send your own tracks that haven't been released or signed anywhere else. You must own all rights, including any samples.

    3. 03

      Finished, full tracks

      Full-length tracks, no previews or snippets. Mastered or a clean premaster is fine.

    4. 04

      Up to 3 tracks

      Pick your strongest work. Upload an MP3 or WAV of any size (big files are shrunk to a high-quality MP3 before sending), or send a private SoundCloud, Dropbox, Google Drive or WeTransfer link that doesn't expire.

    5. 05

      Tell us who you are

      Use the message box: a few lines about you, your music and any past releases or gigs.

    6. 06

      Our reply

      We listen to every demo. If it's a fit, we'll reply by email. Please don't send the same tracks more than once.
