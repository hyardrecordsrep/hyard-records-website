    Send your demo

    Demo guidelines

    Read these before you send. It helps us listen properly.

    1. 01

      Our sound

      Techno, rave, progressive and the electronic music around them. If it would work on a dark dancefloor, we want to hear it.

    2. 02

      Original and unreleased

      Only send your own tracks that haven't been released or signed anywhere else. You must own all rights, including any samples.

    3. 03

      Finished, full tracks

      Full-length tracks, no previews or snippets. Mastered or a clean premaster is fine.

    4. 04

      Up to 3 tracks

      Pick your strongest work. Upload MP3 or WAV files up to 200 MB each, or add a private SoundCloud, Dropbox, Google Drive or WeTransfer link that doesn't expire.

    5. 05

      Tell us who you are

      Use the message box: a few lines about you, your music and any past releases or gigs. Fill in your profile links too.

    6. 06

      Our reply

      We listen to every demo. You can follow its status in your dashboard, and we'll email you whenever it changes. Please don't send the same tracks more than once.

    Send it from your artist account

    Demos are sent from a free Hyard Records artist account. Upload full-quality MP3 or WAV files (up to 200 MB each) and follow your demo from received to signed.
